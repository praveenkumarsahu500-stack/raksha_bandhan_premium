About Raksha Bandhan

Raksha Bandhan is one of the most significant festivals celebrated across India and by Indians worldwide. The word "Raksha" means protection and "Bandhan" means to tie. It is a festival that celebrates the special bond between brothers and sisters. On this auspicious day, sisters tie a sacred thread called "Rakhi" on their brothers' wrists as a symbol of love, care, and prayers for their well-being. In return, brothers pledge to protect and support their sisters throughout their lives.

This premium celebration captures the essence of this beautiful festival with cherished memories and melodious music that resonates with the spirit of togetherness and love.
